ADVISER CONNECT 2026

How we use your event details

Your registration details are used by the event organisers to manage attendance at your chosen lunch, contact you about arrangements, and send a confirmation and event reminder.

What we collect

Your name, email address, practice or organisation, chosen city, dietary requirements, and any optional mobile number or catering notes you provide. Please include only information needed to organise your attendance.

Dietary information

Dietary requirements and allergy information may be shared with the venue or catering team so they can plan your meal. Providing dietary notes is optional.

Storage and email

The registration service uses Google Sheets to store event records. The registration spreadsheet is private and accessible only to the registration account owner. The organiser may prepare reports for the relevant event team. Google Apps Script hosts the registration page and sends confirmations and reminders from ppsregistration@gmail.com. Google may process information outside Australia. Your registration details are emailed to the manager for your chosen state; the public invitation does not show attendee details.

Questions or changes

For a change to your registration, a cancellation, or a request concerning your information, email ppsregistration@gmail.com. Your details are collected for this event; this form does not subscribe you to marketing emails.
