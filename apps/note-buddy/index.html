Note
Buddy

Create meeting notes that combine handwriting, typed text, photos and files. Sync notes across iPhone, iPad and Mac through your private iCloud storage. On supported devices, identify summaries, tasks, owners and due dates using on-device Apple Intelligence.

Meeting notes
and action items

Keep meeting content together in the order you add it. Search your notes and review action items after the meeting.

01

Handwriting and typing

Combine handwritten and typed sections on iPhone and iPad. Apple Pencil is supported on compatible iPads.

02

Calendar integration

With optional Calendar access, a new note can use the current meeting’s title, time and attendees.

03

Private iCloud sync

Sync notes across iPhone, iPad and Mac using your private iCloud storage. ChargeOut does not have access to your notes.

04

Summary and action items

On-device Apple Intelligence can summarise the page and find tasks, owners and due dates on supported devices.

05

Photos and files

Add whiteboards, slides, PDFs and other files. Text recognition makes their contents searchable and useful to the summary.

06

Built for iPhone Duo

Layouts adapt to the outer display, open workspace and propped position. In the propped position, the note appears above the keyboard. Handwriting is read-only on Mac.
