Note
Buddy

Make the most of iPhone Duo with meeting notes that adapt as you close, open or prop up your device. Keep handwriting, typed text, photos and files together, and flag to-dos with an asterisk. Every feature is free, with no note limit. If Note Buddy is useful to you, you can leave an optional beer tip to help cover development costs.

A notebook that adapts
to your iPhone Duo

Use the outer display for a quick note, open Duo for a larger workspace, or prop it on its side with your notes above the keyboard. Your meeting content stays in order as you move between layouts.

01

Handwriting alongside typed notes

Keep handwritten and typed sections on the same scrolling page. Your content stays in place when you switch between writing and the keyboard.

02

Notes linked to your meeting

With optional Calendar access, a new note can use the current meeting’s title, time and attendees.

03

Automatic to-do markers

Start a line or end a paragraph with *, either handwritten or typed, and Note Buddy automatically flags it as a to-do. Use * Send the proposal or Send the proposal * to mark an action while you take notes.

04

Summaries and action items

On-device Apple Intelligence can summarise the page and find tasks, owners and due dates on supported devices.

05

Photos and files in context

Add whiteboards, slides, PDFs and other files. Text recognition makes their contents searchable and useful to the summary.

06

Private sync between your devices

Your notes sync through private iCloud storage, with companion access on iPad and Mac. ChargeOut cannot access your notes. Handwriting is read-only on Mac.
