One page.
Every way you think.

Capture a meeting without sorting your thoughts into separate modes, then find the detail again when it matters.

01

Handwriting and typing

Mix PencilKit handwriting and typed sections in the order you make them on iPhone and iPad.

02

Meeting-aware titles

With optional Calendar access, a new note can use the current meeting’s title, time and attendees.

03

Private iCloud sync

SwiftData mirrors notes to your private iCloud database across your iPhone, iPad and Mac.

04

Summary and action items

On-device Apple Intelligence can summarise the page and find tasks, owners and due dates on supported devices.

05

Photos and files

Add whiteboards, slides, PDFs and other files. Text recognition makes their contents searchable and useful to the summary.

06

Built for iPhone Duo

Compact when closed, roomy when open, and arranged above the fold when propped like a laptop. Handwriting is view-only on Mac.
