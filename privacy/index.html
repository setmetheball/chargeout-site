Website privacy

Effective date: 21 August 2026

Overview

This website does not use advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, user accounts or marketing cookies. It provides product, support and privacy information for ChargeOut Labs apps.

Website hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like other web hosts, GitHub may process routine request information needed to deliver the site and protect its services. ChargeOut Labs does not receive a visitor analytics dashboard from this website.

Contact by email

If you email us, your email provider and ours process the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your enquiry and do not sell it or add it to a marketing list.

App privacy

Each app has its own detailed privacy policy because its on-device features differ. See the privacy page for Work Travel Logbook, Receipt Rescue or Avanti Tracker.

Contact

For privacy questions, email info@chargeout.net.
